How to play

Press and hold anywhere on the canvas, Drag backward(Like pulling a slingshot), Release to throw! A bubble pops up next to your snowman while draggingAngle / Power %a little bubble(It'll automatically pick a spot that won't block the trajectory line), Slingshot colors blue→yellow→orange→red = Power from light to full; Power below 22%: releasing early won't throw (prevents accidental throws). The turn passes automatically after your action. Changed your mind mid-pull?Drag it back to the start and release, Or press Esc, Either one cancels this throw.

⌨️ Keyboard controls(Desktop):↑↓ Adjust the angle, Hold Space to charge, Release to throw. The light tick on the power bar is how hard you threw last time — Fine-tune along it, Much faster than guessing by feel.

🌬 The arrow at the top showsWind direction; 💥 Snowballs blast Snow Pits out of the snow, Can blast a path through mountains.

📏 How this throw is calculated: First check'Distance N units'(One screen = 20 units), Then check the wind correction(Tailwind +, Headwind −). Same distance, same power, Wind correction only adjusts the angle — Play a few rounds and you'll have your own feel table.

🎯 Hits come in three grades, You can tell how well you hit from the visuals and the sound:Direct hit(Dead-on)= The screen stutters for a beat + Heavy shake + Flash and shockwave + Sharp crack;Splash(A graze on the side)= Medium shake + A ground-hugging ring + Dull thud;Miss = A gentle plop + A soft"Puff". The side that got hit:The HP bar leaves a red sliver firstIf it catches up to you again, That red sliver is the HP this throw took off. If it feels too shaky, head to ⚙ Turn off Shake and afterimages in Settings.
