About

Why so smiley?

We are quokkas — the famously smiling marsupials of Rottnest Island, off the coast of Western Australia. In the mid-2010s we went viral as the internet's "world's happiest animal", and the quokka selfie became a global trend — snapped by Chris Hemsworth, Shawn Mendes, Margot Robbie and many more.

From XMC to QKACOIN

QKA was born from XMC mining, in response to where global regulation is heading. To keep the project healthy for the long run, the XMC team listened to the community and the exchange and turned XMC into a meme coin: $QKA. A token with nothing to hide — and a face that won't stop smiling.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you wanted to know about the happiest coin in crypto.

What is QuokkaCoin?
Quokkas are smiley marsupials from Rottnest Island, Western Australia, famous as the internet's "world's happiest animal." QKACOIN ($QKA) is a Solana meme coin built around the quokka spirit — cheerful, community-owned and transparent.
How was QKA born?
$QKA comes from XMC mining. Facing tightening global regulations, the XMC project team respected the views of its community and exchange partners and decided to convert XMC into a community meme coin: QuokkaCoin. A fresh start, same family.
What about the token supply?
The total supply of QKA is 31,668,129 — fully verifiable on the Solana Explorer. It is fixed with no future issuance, and there are no freezing permissions, so every single token can circulate freely.
